# Faceless Islamic Explainer Video (illustration + motion-graphics hybrid)

Reverse-engineered from a 102 s reference (TikTok export, 576×1024, 30 fps, topic: backbiting =
"eating your brother's flesh", Quran 49:12). Use it as the template for making videos in the same
format. Make our own version: our own illustrations, our own wording, our own handle. Don't reuse
the reference creator's watermark, handle, or exact script.

## 1. Format at a glance

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect / res | 9:16, export 1080×1920 (reference was 576×1024), 30 fps |
| Length | ~95 s of content + ~4 s platform outro (≈100 s total) |
| Shots | ~13 shots in total, all **hard cuts** (no crossfades or wipes between shots) |
| Rhythm | Alternates **A: illustrated scene** (dark, cinematic) ↔ **B: cream motion-graphic card** |
| Avg shot length | Illustrations 5–8 s, graphic sections 5–14 s (they hold longer because they build up step by step) |
| Voiceover | One continuous narrator, almost no silence (longest gap ≈0.6 s before the "+0 deeds" reveal) |
| Loudness | ≈ −20 LUFS integrated; aim for −14 LUFS when exporting for TikTok/Reels |
| Speech rate | ≈ 140–150 wpm, calm and deliberate, with short pauses before reveals |

## 2. Narrative flow (beat sheet with timings)

The skeleton is: **Hook → Twist → Evidence → Prophetic story → Definition → Objection → Relatable
scene → Gamified consequence → Emotional climax → Hope → Callback → CTA.**

| # | Time | Shot type | Beat | Reference VO (paraphrase pattern) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules that make this flow work:
- **Hook in 5 s** with a contradiction ("a forbidden meat… not pork"). Open on a mystery object (the
  covered cloche) and show the answer only at beat 4.
- **Each graphic answers the question the last line raised.** No beat repeats information.
- **Evidence always carries a citation pill** (Quran x:y / Sahih Muslim ####). That builds trust.
- **Pre-empt the objection** ("But what if it's true?") before the viewer thinks it.
- **Turn abstract sin into numbers** (sin counter, deed counter, a transfer). That is the retention
  engine in the middle of the video.
- **Low point, then hope**: empty hands → crying in prayer → "door open" → practical takeaway.
- **The ending echoes the opening image** (plate/food ↔ mouth/words). The video should feel like a loop.
- CTA = **"Send this to someone who…"** (a share prompt), shown alone on a clean card.

## 3. Visual style

### A-roll: illustrated scenes
- Semi-realistic **digital painting / anime-cinematic** style. Warm and moody, with strong rim light,
  candles, lamps, god-rays and steam.
- One recurring male protagonist (late 20s, dark wavy hair, beard, olive henley). **Keep the
  character consistent** across every scene (use a character reference / the same seed).
- Settings: candle-lit dining table, sunlit Madinah courtyard (no people shown), night café, barren
  desert under a stormy sky, dim room with an oil lamp. No faces of prophets or companions, ever.
- Motion: each still gets **subtle motion only**: a slow push-in or pull-back (≈5–10 % over the
  shot), gentle parallax, animated steam/smoke/dust/light flicker. Optionally use image-to-video for
  small actions (lifting the cloche, the man walking away, a tear). Never use fast moves.
- Captions are burned in on illustrations only (see §5).

### B-roll: motion-graphic cards
- Background flat cream **#EEE6DB**. Text dark brown/near-black **#342E29**. Accent orange
  **#FD5914**. Good-deeds numbers green (≈#1FA34A). Sin tags red (≈#E5322D). Cards are white
  **#FFFFFF** with a thick black outline and a hard drop shadow (neo-brutalist, offset ≈4 px, no blur).
- Section labels: tiny **uppercase bold sans in a black pill** ("MANY MUSLIMS", "ONLY TWO ARE
  FORBIDDEN", "BACKBITING IS", "YOUR RECORD", "YOUR DEEDS").
- Body/quote text: **condensed serif** (e.g. Playfair Display / Libre Caslon Condensed / Instrument
  Serif), centred, 2–4 lines, keywords in orange.
- Punchlines: **orange italic serif** ("Why?", "You would detest it.").
- UI elements: bold sans (Inter/SF style) inside white rounded cards: rows with icons and "+",
  stamps rotated ≈ −8° ("FORBIDDEN"), dotted connector lines, citation pills (small black
  rounded rectangles with white text).
- Lots of empty space. Content sits in the top ~60 % of the frame (keep it clear of the platform UI
  at the bottom and right).

## 4. Edit and transitions

- **Between shots: hard cuts only**, timed on the start of a VO phrase. A-roll ↔ B-roll every 5–14 s.
- **Inside graphic shots, everything builds on the VO:**
  - Text reveals **word by word in sync with the voice**: each word fades in from ~20 % opacity with
    a light blur (~150 ms). Upcoming words sit faintly visible as "ghost" text before they're spoken.
  - Elements **pop in** with scale 0.9→1.0 plus a small rotation/tilt that settles (≈250 ms, ease-out
    back). New cards slide in from the side with a slight rotation.
  - **Card flip** (Y-axis rotation) for reveals ("?" → "Your brother's flesh").
  - **Stamps slam in** (scale 1.3→1.0, rotated) on the key word ("FORBIDDEN").
  - **Number counters roll** (2×→12×, +0→+1,240, 1,240→310) over ~1 s, synced to VO.
  - **Sequential list build**: rows/checkboxes appear one per beat (M T W T F S S, Prayer → Fasting →
    Charity, sin lines +1 → +4).
  - Desaturate to grey to show loss (the YOU card goes grey when deeds are gone).
- On illustration captions, the **currently spoken word turns orange** (karaoke-style highlight).
- Ending: before the outro, the CTA card **dims to grey** (≈0.3 s) and then cuts to the outro.
- Sound: one soft ambient/piano or nasheed-style vocal pad bed (no instrument-heavy music if the
  audience avoids it) under the VO, ducked about 18 dB below it. Add subtle SFX: whoosh on card entry,
  thud on the stamp, ticks on counters, a soft riser into the Day-of-Judgment shot.

## 5. Captions (illustration shots)

- 2–3 words at a time, **lower third** (~70 % down the frame), centred.
- Font: bold **serif** (e.g. Playfair Display Bold), white with a soft dark shadow, no box.
- The active word is highlighted orange. Phrases break on natural pauses ("There's a meat / the Quran /
  forbids.").
- No captions on cream cards: the on-screen text there *is* the script (or a visual metaphor for it).

## 6. Voice

- One calm, intimate, sincere male narrator. Low-mid pitch, warm, slightly breathy, close-mic.
  Think "a brother advising you", not a preacher shouting.
- Pace ≈145 wpm. **Pause before reveals** ("And no… it's not pork."; "your brother's… flesh";
  "Judgment… and your hands are empty"). Slow down on verses and hadith.
- Use direct address ("you", "your brother", "your hands"). Keep sentences short, and mix in
  rhetorical questions.
- Say the honorific after the Prophet's name (ﷺ is shown on screen too).
- Arabic words are pronounced correctly and then translated ("lahm, meat").
- If you use TTS (e.g. ElevenLabs), pick a deep, warm, slow narrator voice with stability around 50 %
  and moderate style. Add manual pauses with punctuation/ellipses.

## 7. Script template (fill in for a new topic)

```
HOOK (0–5s, A-roll mystery image):  "There's a <X> the <source> forbids/warns about. And no — it's not <obvious guess>."
STAKES (B):    "<Many people> do it <every single day>, without noticing."
FACT (B):      <surprising stat / Arabic word + meaning + count>
REVEAL (B):    "Only <n> are <…>: <obvious one>… and <the twist>."  (card flip)
EVIDENCE (B):  <verse word-by-word> + citation pill + italic punchline
STORY (A):     "The Prophet ﷺ once asked: '<question>?' Then he said:"
DEFINE (B):    <hadith definition> + citation
OBJECTION (B): "'But what if <excuse>?'" → split cards <case 1> / <case 2>
RELATABLE (A): "<X> is easy. <How it happens>. And you think, 'What's the big deal?'"
GAMIFY (B):    record/counter of the sin  → counter of good deeds
CLIMAX (A):    "Then you stand before Allah on the Day of Judgment… and <loss>."
WHY (B):       mechanism visualised with numbers + citation
PAIN (A):      "<Years of …>, <lost> over <something small>."
HOPE (B):      "But the same <verse/hadith> leaves a door open:" + mercy verse
CALLBACK (A):  "You're careful about <hook image>. Be just as careful about <the lesson>."
CTA (B):       "Send this to someone who needs to hear it."
```
Aim for 230–260 words, 95 s.

## 8. Production checklist

1. Script from the template; double-check every verse/hadith number against an authentic source.
2. Record or generate the VO first, then get word timestamps (e.g. Whisper `word_timestamps=True`).
3. Generate the illustrations (same character sheet, 9:16, warm cinematic lighting) and animate them
   lightly.
4. Build the cream cards (After Effects / CapCut / Remotion / Motion Canvas) using the palette and
   fonts above, with word-by-word reveals keyed to the VO timestamps.
5. Cut A/B on phrase starts, add the music bed + SFX, burn in the karaoke captions on A-roll.
6. Export 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264, −14 LUFS. Keep the safe zones clear (bottom ~20 %, right ~15 %).
7. Review it against §2: is the hook under 5 s, does every evidence card have a citation, does the
   ending call back to the opening, and is the share CTA there?

## 9. Analysing a new reference video (how this was made)

```bash
V=ref.mov
ffprobe -v error -show_entries format=duration:stream=codec_type,width,height,r_frame_rate -of compact "$V"
ffmpeg -i "$V" -filter:v "select='gt(scene,0.2)',showinfo" -f null - 2>&1 | grep -o "pts_time:[0-9.]*"   # cut list
mkdir f && ffmpeg -i "$V" -vf "fps=2,scale=240:-1,drawtext=text='%{pts\:flt}':x=4:y=4:fontsize=18:fontcolor=yellow:box=1" f/%03d.jpg
ffmpeg -start_number 1 -i f/%03d.jpg -vf tile=10x3 -frames:v 1 sheet1.jpg      # contact sheets → read captions/graphics
ffmpeg -ss <cut-0.3> -i "$V" -frames:v 10 -vf "scale=144:-1,tile=10x1" cut.jpg # frame-by-frame view of a transition
ffmpeg -i "$V" -af silencedetect=noise=-35dB:d=0.3 -f null -                   # VO pauses
ffmpeg -i "$V" -af ebur128 -f null - 2>&1 | grep "I:"                          # loudness
```
Add the findings to this skill as a new section, or as a variant of §2, when a reference adds a new
pattern.
